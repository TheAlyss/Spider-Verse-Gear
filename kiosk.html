Raid Supply Rules

Figures are the supplies ONE hero uses in ONE day.

1. Boss Tier Supply Rates (Per Hero / Per Day)

Tier Med-Gel Web-Fluid Shield Charge
Easy 3 2 1
Normal 6 4 2
Hard 9 6 3
Very Hard 12 8 4
Hardcore 15 10 5

(Each tier up adds +3 Med-Gel, +2 Web-Fluid, +1 Shield Charge. Higher tiers are tougher bosses, so each hero needs more healing, web ammunition and shield energy.)

2. Raid Strategy Modifier (Per Hero / Per Day)

  • Balanced: No extra supplies needed.
  • Stealth: +2 Web-Fluid per hero/day for silent takedowns and high perches.
  • All-Out Assault: +2 Med-Gel and +1 Shield Charge per hero/day for frontline combat.

3. Equipment Item Unit Prices

  • Med-Gel: RM 5.00 / unit
  • Web-Fluid: RM 8.00 / unit
  • Shield Charge: RM 15.00 / unit

4. One-Time Squad Power-Ups (Per Hero)

  • Revive Serum: RM 60.00 each
  • Web-Shooter Upgrade: RM 80.00 each
  • Spider-Armor Plating: RM 100.00 each

5. Tactical Allocation Formula

  • Per hero per day = Tier figure + Strategy extra
  • Each hero carries = Per hero per day × Number of days
  • Squad total = Each hero carries × Number of heroes
  • Total Cost = (Squad total × Item price) + (Power-up price × Number of heroes)
